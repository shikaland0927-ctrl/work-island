### Description

Work Island is a private, local-first work timer for macOS.

Move the pointer to the top of your display to reveal quick controls, then start, pause, resume, finish, or safely discard an activity recording without interrupting your flow.

Organize reusable activities, add notes, enter time manually, and review your progress with a heatmap, activity-colored graphs, distribution charts, and a complete history.

Your activities, notes, and records stay on your Mac. Work Island has no account, analytics, advertising, or cloud tracking.

Features:

- Hover-based top-of-screen timer controls
- Reusable activities and record notes
- Manual time entry
- Heatmap, graph, and activity distribution views
- Complete editable history
- Local backup import and export
- Local-only storage

### Review Notes

Work Island intentionally runs as a background notch utility. Its compact black panel is centered at the top edge of the current display. Hover over or click that panel to reveal timer controls. Use the Window symbol in the panel to open the main window; Quit remains available from the standard Work Island application menu. Launch at Login can be enabled during setup or in Settings. The Dock and Command-Tab icon appears only while the main window or Settings is visible.

No account or sample credentials are required. All data is stored locally in the app sandbox. Import and Export access only a JSON file explicitly selected by the user.
